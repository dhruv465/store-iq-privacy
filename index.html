Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy covers Store IQ, a WhatsApp copilot operated by Dhruv Sathe for store associates. Associates use it to look up product information and prepare customer conversations. It is not a customer-facing service.

Store IQ is an independent prototype and is not an official service of Reliance Retail or Reliance Digital. Do not send payment details, passwords, identity documents, or other information that is not needed to help with a store conversation.

Information processed

  • Associate account and messages: WhatsApp phone number, available profile name, message text, button selections, timestamps, message identifiers, and delivery or error status.
  • Customer details shared by an associate: information about a customer's needs, home, budget, preferences, product choices, questions, and the store visit. These details may identify a customer if an associate includes identifying information in a message.
  • Voice notes and photos: audio or product-label photos an associate sends so Store IQ can transcribe speech or read a label. Voice transcripts and relevant extracted text can be kept with the interaction.
  • Store operations: associate roster and names, interaction summaries, scorecard and product-choice information, expert questions, store-visit notes, floor notices, and records needed to process duplicate messages and troubleshoot delivery.

How information is used

Store IQ uses information to answer associates, find catalogue products, keep a working interaction card, prepare a customer summary for the associate to review, track store operations, and deliver replies and manager notices. A customer summary is sent only after the associate records the customer's consent. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

AI systems may misunderstand text, speech or photos. Associates should review information before sharing a reply or summary with a customer.

Services that process information

  • WhatsApp and Meta carry associate messages and Store IQ replies. Meta provides message content, phone numbers, profile information and message identifiers needed to deliver the service.
  • Google Gemini processes message context when used to answer, and processes voice notes or label photos for transcription or reading. When configured, fal.ai routes text requests to the selected AI model and transcribes voice notes using Whisper. fal.ai temporarily receives the audio file for transcription.
  • Reliance Digital catalogue services receive product search terms and catalogue queries to return product information. Store IQ does not intentionally send associate phone numbers or customer identity details in catalogue searches.
  • The Store IQ operator's server stores operational records in a local database while the prototype runs. The operator can access records for support, security, manager features and privacy requests. The webhook may be exposed through a secure tunnel service while the prototype is running.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices. Their handling, location and retention may differ from Store IQ's. See the WhatsApp Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy and fal.ai Privacy Policy.

Storage and retention

Store IQ currently runs as a prototype on an operator-managed server. Its local database keeps message-processing records, replies, interaction state, roster and manager records, and relevant operational history on disk. There is no automatic deletion schedule for these records. The operator may clear or replace the local database as part of operating the prototype.

Audio and image bytes are processed temporarily by the application and are not stored as media files in its local database. WhatsApp and AI providers may retain message or media copies under their own policies. Voice transcripts and text extracted from photos may remain in interaction records.

Deleting a WhatsApp chat on a device does not delete records held by Store IQ or its providers.

Request access, correction or deletion

Associates and people whose information was shared with Store IQ may request access, correction or deletion by email. To help locate records, include the WhatsApp phone number used for the interaction, approximate date, and the request you want handled. Do not email passwords, verification codes or identity documents.

  1. Email homescience506@gmail.com with the subject Store IQ privacy request.
  2. We may ask for reasonable confirmation to make sure the request concerns your information or that you are authorized to make it.
  3. The operator will review records under Store IQ's control and respond about the action taken. Some records may remain where needed for security, duplicate prevention or legal obligations. This request does not delete copies held by WhatsApp, AI providers or other services; contact those providers for requests about their records.

Requests are handled manually. Sending a new message after a deletion may create new records.
